SECTION 7. DIAGNOSTIC TESTING — SLEEP DISORDERS
==========================================================================

7.1  COVERED SERVICES
The following diagnostic services for suspected obstructive sleep apnea
(OSA) are covered when ordered by a treating physician:

  (a) Home Sleep Apnea Testing (HSAT) — CPT 95800, 95801, 95806.
  (b) Attended In-Lab Polysomnography (PSG) — CPT 95810, 95811.
  (c) Multiple Sleep Latency Testing — CPT 95805.

7.2  MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA FOR IN-LAB POLYSOMNOGRAPHY (CPT 95810)
In-lab polysomnography is considered medically necessary when ANY of
the following criteria are met:

  (i)   The member has a comorbid condition that renders HSAT
        unreliable, including but not limited to: moderate-to-severe
        pulmonary disease, congestive heart failure (NYHA Class III
        or IV), neuromuscular disease, or chronic opioid use.
  (ii)  HSAT has been attempted and returned a non-diagnostic or
        technically inadequate result.
  (iii) The treating physician documents a clinical suspicion of a
        non-respiratory sleep disorder (e.g., parasomnia, periodic
        limb movement disorder, narcolepsy) that cannot be evaluated
        by HSAT.
  (iv)  The member is unable to operate HSAT equipment safely at home
        due to cognitive or physical impairment.

7.3  PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Prior authorization is NOT required for in-lab polysomnography when
ordered by a board-certified sleep medicine physician, pulmonologist,
or neurologist and one or more of the criteria in 7.2 are documented
in the member's medical record.

SECTION 12. APPEALS
==========================================================================

12.1  Members have 180 days from the date of an adverse benefit
determination to file a first-level internal appeal. Appeals must be
submitted in writing and should reference the claim number, the
specific denial reason, and any clinical or contractual grounds for
reversal.

12.2  The plan will issue a written decision within 30 days of receipt
of a complete appeal.
